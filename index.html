About Me

Who Am I?

+

Sebastian Kvist — a 31-year-old born in Oskarshamn, but now happily resides in Täby, Stockholm, with my partner and dog.

I'm currently studying to become a .NET Web Developer at Jönköping University, Campus Värnamo. Most of my time has gone into learning C# and .NET, but I've also worked with frontend development using mostly React. During our studies we've also explored other tools and languages like TypeScript, which has given me a good mix of backend and frontend experience.

What is your background?

+

Before diving into programming, I completed a bachelor's degree in Graphic Design and Communication at Linköping University. While it might seem like a different path, my design background has been a huge asset in my journey as a programmer.

What is your approach to programming?

+

I try to always welcome every error and bug with a mind of a learner. I would also say that understanding design principles has helped me approach problems with a focus on user experience, which I'd say is key when creating intuitive applications. This perspective makes me more mindful of how people interact with software, and it helps me better understand what my code actually should achieve.

Future Goals?

+

Of course, I'm always eager to learn new things, whether it's a new language or a new tool. Most of all, I look forward to growing my skills so that I can offer my expertise to others and, in my own way, contribute to making life a little better, no matter how small the impact may be.
